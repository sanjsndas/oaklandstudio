Privacy Policy

Oakline Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal data when you visit our website, contact us or use our interior design services in England.

  1. Who we are Oakline Studio is the organisation responsible for your personal data (the data controller). If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your data, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

  2. Personal data we collect Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

    • Contact details, such as your name, email address, telephone number and address.
    • Project information, such as your property details, design preferences, plans, photographs, budget and communications with us.
    • Transaction information relating to services you purchase from us.
    • Technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and information about how you use our website.
    • Any other information you choose to provide.

  3. How we use your data We may use your personal data to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver interior design services, manage projects and payments, communicate with you, maintain our business records, improve our website and services, and meet legal obligations.

  4. Our lawful bases for processing Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract with you, complying with legal obligations, pursuing our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, and your consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.

  5. Sharing your data We may share personal data with service providers who help us operate our business, such as website hosting, IT, payment, accounting or project-support providers. We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our legal rights. We do not sell your personal data. Where a service provider processes data on our behalf, we require it to protect that data and use it only for authorised purposes.

  6. International transfers If personal data is transferred outside the UK, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to ensure it is protected, such as using an approved transfer mechanism where necessary.

  7. How long we keep your data We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide our services, maintain appropriate business and financial records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

  8. Your rights Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal data, have inaccurate data corrected, request erasure or restriction of processing, object to certain processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise your rights, contact us using the details provided on our website. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

  9. Cookies and website analytics Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make the site work, remember preferences and understand website usage. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, our cookie controls. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

  10. Security We use appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  11. Children's privacy Our services and website are not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children in connection with them.

  12. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on our website with its updated date.
